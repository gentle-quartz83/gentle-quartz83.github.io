End In Stone Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026.

End In Stone keeps one length of dry-stone wall on this iPhone. End the tail into the wall, heart the course with flat stones, and tie both faces with a throughstone. A stone that reaches the far face comes back. Round fill rolls out and does not heart the course. The app does not collect data, does not require an account, and has no purchases.

Contact address: United States.
