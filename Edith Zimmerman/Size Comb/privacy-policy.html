Size Comb Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026.

Size Comb keeps size-bath sheets on this iPhone. Drop beads, pull the wide comb across and back, then use the fine comb to write nonpareil. A lifted sheet can be renamed. The app does not collect data, does not require an account, and has no purchases.
