Yarn Vat Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026.

Yarn Vat keeps indigo hanks on this iPhone. Wet a dry hank, dip it until it comes out green, then air it until the green is gone before the next shade. At shade 3 the hank stays and can be renamed. The app does not collect data, does not require an account, and has no purchases.

Contact address: United States.
