Brick Shove Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026.

Brick Shove keeps one brick and its head joint on this iPhone. Butter the end, then shove the brick until mortar squeezes out of the top of the joint. Slushing an open head does not fill it. Tool the joint only when it is at thumbprint. Moving a brick that has already been laid throws that brick away. The app does not collect data, does not require an account, and has no purchases.

Contact address: United States.
