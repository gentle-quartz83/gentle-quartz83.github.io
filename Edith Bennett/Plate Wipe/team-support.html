Plate Wipe Support

Plate Wipe is a free iPhone app for warming a copper plate and wiping its ink. There is no account and nothing to buy.

Hold the heater until the plate is warm. A short tap does not warm it. A cold wipe leaves the ink. A warm wipe clears the face and drops the line one grade. Pull saves the sentence, and a later pull replaces it.
