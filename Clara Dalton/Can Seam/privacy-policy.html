Can Seam Privacy Policy

Clara Dalton

Can Seam keeps each can on this iPhone. The end seats only when it reaches the chuck. One notch on the first roll locks the hooks. A second notch crushes the curl, and a crushed can cannot be sealed. The iron roll slips before the hooks lock. After they lock, one notch of the iron roll seals the seam. The app does not collect data, does not use an account, and does not include purchases. Sharing uses the system share sheet and only sends the can name and whether the seam is sealed, crushed, or not sealed.
